# Competitor Landscape

Status: Draft, unverified. Written on 2026-10-30 from secondary sources (comparison blogs and news articles written by vendors and reviewers). Prices and features were not confirmed on each vendor's own site and several sources disagree. Re-check every row on the vendor's official pages before using it in a decision or in product copy.

## 1. Groups

| Group | Examples | What they do | Reported pricing (unverified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trackers and resume tools | Teal, Huntr, Careerflow, Jobscan, Simplify | Track applications, tailor resumes, scan keywords, autofill forms | Teal about 13 USD per week or 29 USD per month. Huntr about 40 USD per month. Jobscan about 50 USD per month. Simplify core free with a paid tier. Careerflow figures conflict between sources |
| Platform AI | LinkedIn Job Match and Premium Career | Shows how a profile lines up against a posting | Sources disagree on what is free and what needs Premium |
| India job boards | Naukri, Internshala, Instahyre, Hirect | Own job inventory and the fresher audience | Internshala mostly free with paid add-ons. Naukri paid tiers. Others unclear |
| Auto-apply tools | myjobb.ai and similar | Apply to jobs on the user's behalf | From about 499 INR per month for one reported tool |

## 2. What this implies

- US trackers and scanners are generally described as keyword based. Whether their matching is weaker than evidence-based matching is our reading of marketing pages, not a verified claim.
- Indian boards own the inventory and much of it is free to seekers. The product cannot win on inventory.
- LinkedIn already offers fit feedback inside the place people browse. The product has to win on evidence, honesty and what happens after the verdict.
- Auto-apply is a different philosophy. The product never submits applications (ADR-0005).

## 3. Differentiators the product is built around

1. Every verdict cites evidence that code can verify.
2. Honest source labeling, including what was imported by the user.
3. Gap-to-qualify plans for near-miss jobs.
4. A resume studio with real formatting freedom and grounded suggestions.
5. Application text drafts tied to the user's real evidence, edited and sent by the user.
